This is the staff side of Sweet Savor Cafe. It's built to keep your kitchen calm, even when the orders aren't.

Getting in is simple, and secure. Tap Staff, and a quick PIN unlocks the screens. Behind that, each phone signs in once with an authorized staff account, and that's what connects it to your shared database. The PIN alone doesn't open the database. Take a moment to check Settings and confirm you're connected, because that's what keeps orders and menu changes in step across every phone.

Now, the heart of it all. Orders. The moment a customer places an order, it lands right here, and you may hear a chime. Three views keep you organized: Active, Ready, and Done today. Each card gives you the order number, the customer's name and phone number, every item with its quantity, options, extras, and notes, and the total. And any cash order that hasn't been paid carries a bold red Unpaid tag, so nobody forgets to collect.

When the kitchen begins, tap Start preparing. When the food is done, tap Ready, notify customer. The status updates right away, and the customer's app can show an alert. Push alerts depend on the customer's device and permission. If they asked for a text, your phone opens a message that's ready to go. You look it over, and you send it yourself. Missed them? Tap Send alert again. For a cash order, collect the amount shown and hand over the meal. Orders that are already paid just need Picked up. Either way, the order moves to Done today. One note. In this preview, PayPal orders are simulated, so they aren't settled payments.

Next up, your menu, in Menu Manager. When something runs out, flip its switch to sold out, and customers see it right away. When it's back, flip it back. Tap any item to edit its name, description, photo, price, category, and badge. Show a sale with an old price and a sale tag. Choose the options customers can pick, and decide which sides, drinks, sauces, and extras appear under Complete the Plate. Adding something new takes seconds. Tap Add meal, or Add extra. Save, and it's published to your shared backend. Delete always asks you to confirm first. And a quick tip for photos: use a public image web address, so every customer can see it.

Settings puts the whole cafe at your fingertips. Slammed? Pause online ordering. Customers can still browse, but they can't place orders, and you can resume with one tap. Update your cafe's name, tagline, logo, and colors. Change the address, the phone number, and each day's pickup hours. Set your tax rate. And manage rewards. Turn them on, choose how many orders earn one, set the points per dollar, and name the reward. Right now, the reward takes the price of the cheapest side off the order. You'll also manage your staff PIN and Sales passcode here, and those stay on this device. Changes apply as you make them. And Reset to defaults is there for demos. It asks first, so nothing is lost by accident.

Last, Sales. It has its own passcode, and it locks again as soon as you leave. Switch between Today and This week, and you'll see total sales with tax, the number of orders, your average order, and a chart. See how customers paid, with PayPal or cash. And scroll down to find your best-selling meals and add-ons. These figures summarize the orders that were recorded, so unpaid cash and preview PayPal orders are included. Think of it as a snapshot to help you plan, not an accounting report.

That's the staff side. Customers order, you prepare, everyone gets notified, your menu stays current, and your numbers stay close. When you're finished, exit staff mode, and the app is ready for the next guest.
